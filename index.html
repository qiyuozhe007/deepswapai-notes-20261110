Is there a video face swap tool I can try without signing up?

Short answer: yes. Deep Swap AI's face swap video tool lets creators and casual users replace a face in a short clip with free tries, and basic use does not require registration.

Most people asking this want to test a video face swap on a meme, a birthday clip or a short social post before committing to any account. Deep Swap AI is built for that first try: upload a clip, add a face photo, and get a swapped result in the browser.

What makes it different

  • Free tries on the free tier, with no sign-up needed for basic use.
  • Multi-face support: the site says it can handle up to 6 people in one clip.
  • HD and 4K output are listed on the official site.
